### JUSTICE-KP

<a href="https://v-exor.github.io/Justice_KP_Pages/">
  <picture>
    <source media="(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)" srcset="./assets/project-justice.png">
    <img src="./assets/project-justice.gif" alt="JUSTICE-KP — Experimental Research / Thesis. Transcription-assisted decision support for Katarungang Pambarangay." width="100%">
  </picture>
</a>

Developed during our **experimental research / thesis**, JUSTICE-KP supports hearing transcription and case workflows for **Katarungang Pambarangay**.

- Local speech transcription, speaker identification, and editable transcripts.
- Rule-based case analysis, jurisdiction checks, and sensitive-case referral routing.
- Hearing schedules, evidence, KP form workflows, and research validation tools.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS` `Python` `FastAPI` `faster-whisper`

[Research website ↗](https://v-exor.github.io/Justice_KP_Pages/) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [System source](https://github.com/v-Exor/JUSTICE-KP-System) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Website source](https://github.com/v-Exor/Justice_KP_Pages)

<sub>The research website is separate from the system application. The system supports decisions; final authority remains with barangay officials.</sub>

### GestureDeck

<a href="https://github.com/v-Exor/GestureDeck_CV_Project">
  <picture>
    <source media="(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)" srcset="./assets/project-gesture.png">
    <img src="./assets/project-gesture.gif" alt="GestureDeck — Computer Vision. Hand-gesture control for Windows PowerPoint." width="100%">
  </picture>
</a>

A **CSST 106 Computer Vision** classroom prototype for controlling a Windows PowerPoint slideshow through webcam-based hand gestures. It combines MediaPipe recognition with local geometric and temporal rules.

- Palm-to-back and back-to-palm turns navigate slides.
- Held fist and Victory gestures pause, resume, or exit slideshow control.
- Hand-landmark preview, recognition feedback, and optional local recording.

`Python` `MediaPipe` `OpenCV`

[Source code](https://github.com/v-Exor/GestureDeck_CV_Project) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Setup & documentation](https://github.com/v-Exor/GestureDeck_CV_Project#readme)

<sub>A classroom demonstration prototype; fast turns can produce missed commands.</sub>
